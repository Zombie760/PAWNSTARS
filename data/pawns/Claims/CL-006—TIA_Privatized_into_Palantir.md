# CL-006 — TIA Privatized into Palantir by Thiel

CLAIM ID: CL-006
CLAIM: Total Information Awareness (TIA) — John Poindexter's flagship surveillance program at DARPA's Information Awareness Office — was not actually shut down but privatized into Palantir Technologies; Peter Thiel incorporated Palantir in May 2003 (same month TIA renamed "Terrorism Information Awareness"); CIA In-Q-Tel invested ~$2M (first outside capital); Palantir engineers made 200+ trips to CIA HQ 2005-2009; CEO Alex Karp publicly acknowledged "the real value of the In-Q-Tel investment was that it gave Palantir access to the CIA analysts who were its intended clients."
CLAIM-STYLE: Inductive (corporate incorporation records + In-Q-Tel investment + CIA client relationship + Poindexter meeting + technical overlap).
EVIDENCE:
  1. Palantir incorporation Delaware May 2003 / source: https://web.archive.org/web/20140516035733/http:/www.mausstrategicconsulting.com/1/post/2014/04/a-pretty-complete-history-of-palantir.html / falsifier: If Palantir incorporation date ≠ May 2003, claim fails.
  2. TIA renamed May 2003 (same month) / source: https://www.aclu.org/other/qa-pentagons-total-information-awareness-program / falsifier: If TIA rename ≠ May 2003, claim weakens.
  3. Richard Perle introduced Thiel/Karp to Poindexter / source: https://nymag.com/intelligencer/2020/09/inside-palantir-technologies-peter-thiel-alex-karp.html / falsifier: If meeting never occurred, claim weakens.
  4. In-Q-Tel investment ~$2M, publicly disclosed mid-2006 / source: https://web.archive.org/web/20140516035733/http:/www.mausstrategicconsulting.com/1/post/2014/04/a-pretty-complete-history-of-palantir.html / falsifier: If no In-Q-Tel investment, claim fails.
  5. CIA sole client 2003-2008; Aki Jain 200+ CIA HQ trips / source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2020/10/21/magazine/palantir-alex-karp.html / falsifier: If CIA not sole client, claim fails.
  6. Karp NYT quote: "the real value of the In-Q-Tel investment was that it gave Palantir access to the CIA analysts who were its intended clients" / source: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2020/10/21/magazine/palantir-alex-karp.html / falsifier: If quote not in NYT, claim fails.
  7. Technical overlap: "selective revelation" + "immutable log" = direct TIA borrow / source: https://unlimitedhangout.com/2020/12/investigative-series/palantirs-tiberius-race-and-the-public-health-panopticon/ / falsifier: If no technical overlap, claim weakens.
ARGUMENT: TIA was publicly defunded by Congress in late 2003. The same month, Thiel incorporated Palantir. Poindexter (TIA architect) met with Thiel/Karp. In-Q-Tel (CIA venture) became first investor. CIA was sole client for 5 years. Palantir's core features mirror TIA's design. This is privatization by another name.
