# PR-003 — LifeLog

**DESCRIPTION:** DARPA program (NOT under IAO, but parallel). PM Douglas Gage (Poindexter's close friend). Designed to "build a database tracking a person's entire existence" including communications (phone, mail), media consumption, purchases, location, biometrics. "Everything an individual says, sees, or does." Mapped "relationships, memories, events and experiences." Promoted as creating "next-generation digital assistants" with "near-perfect digital memory." Crit: Lee Tien (EFF): programs "have obvious, easy paths to Homeland Security deployments." Wired quoted Steven Aftergood: "LifeLog has the potential to become something like 'TIA cubed.'" Douglas Gage later told VICE: "Facebook is the real face of pseudo-LifeLog at this point."

**CANCELLED:** February 4, 2004 — same day Facebook launched.
