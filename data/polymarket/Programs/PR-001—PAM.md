# PR-001 — Policy Analysis Market (PAM)

**DESCRIPTION:** DARPA prediction market program, part of the Information Awareness Office (IAO), run by John Poindexter. 2001: DARPA issued request for proposals for "Electronic Market-Based Decision Support." Contracts awarded to Neoteric Technologies (subcontracted to Martek, IEM architects) and Net Exchange (Hanson/Porter from George Mason). PAM used combinatorial market techniques. Initial plan: have intelligence agency employees trade. No agency showed interest. Opened to select members of public. Test operations Sept 2003, full launch Jan 2004. July 2003: branded "Terrorism Futures Market" by Sen. Wyden/Dorgan press conference. Pentagon abandoned July 29, 2003. Congress cut IAO funding entirely in Sept 2003.

**DESIGNERS:** Robin Hanson (system architect), David Porter (George Mason). Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU) provided data. Tradesports/InTrade (John Delaney CEO) consulted.

**PAM LOGO:** Pyramid (one-eyed, surveilling Earth) within letter A, beam touching market graph — directly mirrors TIA logo.

**FOCUS:** 8 Middle Eastern nations (Turkey, Syria, Israel, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, Jordan, Egypt). 5 parameters per nation (US financial involvement, US military activity, economic growth, political instability, military activity). Traders could trade futures on future events in those nations.
