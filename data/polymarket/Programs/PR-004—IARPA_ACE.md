# PR-004 — IARPA Aggregative Contingent Estimation (ACE)

**DESCRIPTION:** DARPA's sister agency IARPA program, $50M, 4-year duration. Goal: "pay people willing to predict major world events." Headed by Jason Matheny. Officially ended June 2015. After ACE, Matheny ran IARPA, then launched Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET) at Georgetown with $55M grant from Open Philanthropy Project (co-founded by Dustin Moskovitz). ACE ended shortly after Joey Krug formalized the Forecast Foundation (Augur) in Estonia, Dec 2014.
