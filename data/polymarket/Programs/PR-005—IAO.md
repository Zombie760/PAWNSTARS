# PR-005 — Information Awareness Office (IAO)

**DESCRIPTION:** DARPA office created at the behest of John Poindexter, who was subsequently hired as DARPA executive and put in charge. Housed TIA (flagship), PAM (via Foster's FutureMAP), LifeLog (parallel/related), and various other DARPA programs. IAO programs were designed to "interface" — to be directly connected to TIA and to each other "in order to improve the accuracy of predictions." Defunded entirely by Congress in legislation signed September 2003. Programs covertly divided among military/IC agencies, with TIA's core software continuing at Palantir.
