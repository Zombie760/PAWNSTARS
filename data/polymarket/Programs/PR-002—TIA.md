# PR-002 — Total Information Awareness (TIA)

**DESCRIPTION:** DARPA surveillance dragnet program, Poindexter's "pre-crime" program. Designed to develop predictive applications for national security and law enforcement. Used mass surveillance of Americans' daily activities. Feb 2003: formally launched. May 2003: renamed Terrorism Information Awareness. By end of 2003: shuttered. But "TIA was never actually shut down despite being defunded by Congress." Various programs "covertly divided" among US military/intelligence agencies. Core panopticon software went to Palantir.

**TIA LOGO:** One-eyed pyramid surveilling Earth, flanked by Latin "scientia est potentia" (science is power).
