# PE-017 — Joey Krug

**DESCRIPTION:** Born 1995. Started mining Bitcoin May 2011 (age 15). Co-founded Augur 2014 (Thiel Fellow, with Jeremy Gardner, Jack Peterson). Advised by Vitalik Buterin (Thiel Fellow) and Robin Hanson (PAM architect). Augur's logo deliberately evokes TIA/PAM pyramid. **Thiel Fellowship June 2016** to develop Augur. Joined Pantera Capital as co-CIO 2017. Forecast Foundation founded Estonia Dec 2014. Augur REP ICO Fall 2015 (first on Ethereum). Left Augur amid messy lawsuit. **Joined Peter Thiel's Founders Fund April 2023 with Sean Parker to shape crypto strategy.** Led $45M Polymarket investment round 2024. December 2025: "maybe Augur is Napster and Polymarket is Spotify."
