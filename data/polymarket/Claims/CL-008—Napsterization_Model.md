# CL-008 — "Napsterization" model as Thiel network disruption pattern

**DESCRIPTION:** A documented pattern in the Thiel network of companies (Parker/Zuckerberg/Thiel with Facebook, Kalanick with Uber, Coplan with Polymarket) follows a "Napsterization" model: (1) launch in regulatory gray area or outright illegal service, (2) get raided/fined/sued, (3) eventually secure de facto regulatory capture, (4) "change the rules of their regulators." Napster (Parker) failed; Spotify (Parker at Founders Fund) succeeded. Augur (Krug) failed; Polymarket (Krug at Founders Fund) succeeded. Coplan gave a 2018 speech titled "the Napsterization of Finance" and later claimed Polymarket was at the vanguard of "the napsterization of finance." Forbes quoted Zuckerberg: "Sean was pivotal in helping Facebook transform from a college project into a real company."
