# CL-003 — LifeLog Launched as Facebook Same Day (Feb 4 2004)

CLAIM ID: CL-003
CLAIM: DARPA LifeLog program (PM Douglas Gage, Poindexter's close friend) was publicly cancelled on February 4, 2004; on the SAME DAY, Facebook launched at Harvard. Douglas Gage later told VICE: "Facebook is the real face of pseudo-LifeLog at this point."
CLAIM-STYLE: Inductive (DARPA cancellation date + Facebook launch date + Gage quote + Thiel/Parker early involvement).
EVIDENCE:
  1. Wired 2004: "Pentagon Kills LifeLog Project" — cancelled Feb 4 2004 / source: https://www.wired.com/2004/02/pentagon-kills-lifelog-project/ / falsifier: If LifeLog cancellation date ≠ Feb 4 2004, claim fails.
  2. Facebook launch date: Feb 4 2004 (TheFacebook.com at Harvard) / source: https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/facebook-launches-mark-zuckerberg / falsifier: If Facebook launch date ≠ Feb 4 2004, claim fails.
  3. Gage VICE quote: "Facebook is the real face of pseudo-LifeLog at this point" / source: https://www.vice.com/en/article/vbqdb8/15-years-ago-the-military-tried-to-record-whole-human-lives-it-ended-badly / falsifier: If Gage never said this, claim weakens.
  4. Thiel $500K Facebook investment Sep 2004, joined board / source: https://www.nytimes.com/2022/02/07/technology/peter-thiel-facebook.html / falsifier: If Thiel investment date ≠ Sep 2004, claim weakens.
  5. Sean Parker (CIA-recruited at 16) became Facebook president June 2004 / source: https://web.archive.org/web/20120614080645/http://www.forbes.com/sites/stevenbertoni/2011/09/21/sean-parker-agent-of-disruption/4/ / falsifier: If Parker not Facebook president, claim weakens.
ARGUMENT: LifeLog aimed to "build a database tracking a person's entire existence" — communications, media consumption, purchases, location, biometrics. Facebook became exactly that. Cancelled and launched same day. Gage admits it. Thiel (already privatizing TIA as Palantir) and Parker (CIA-recruited) were early Facebook insiders. This is privatization pattern #2.
