# CL-007 — Sean Parker's intelligence lineage: FBI, CIA, MK-ULTRA

**DESCRIPTION:** Sean Parker, Facebook's first president, was raided by the FBI at age 15 for hacking government/corporate databases. The CIA then "recruited him at the age of sixteen" after a computer science fair. Parker's father, Bruce Parker Jr., worked at NOAA (1974-2004) including as chief scientist of National Ocean Service, and was involved in **Project Medea** — a CIA data-sharing effort spearheaded by VP Al Gore. His father's boss at NOAA (D. James Baker) was on Project Medea's team. Bruce Parker Jr. later joined the Edge Foundation, "founded by John Brockman and largely financed by Jeffrey Epstein." Parker's grandfather, Bruce Parker Sr. (water skiing pioneer), partnered his water ski schools with Harry Conover, whose wife **Candy Jones** "became the earliest MK-ULTRA whistle-blower years before the Church Committee exposed the CIA project's existence."
