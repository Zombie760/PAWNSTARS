# CL-001 — PAM is Polymarket Direct Ancestor

CLAIM ID: CL-001
CLAIM: DARPA's Policy Analysis Market (PAM) — part of the Information Awareness Office under John Poindexter — was the direct architectural ancestor of Polymarket; Shayne Coplan emailed PAM system architect Robin Hanson in 2019 stating he wanted "to be the person to bring prediction markets to life" and Polymarket launched in 2020 with Thiel-linked funding.
CLAIM-STYLE: Inductive (DARPA PAM documentation + Coplan-Hanson email + Polymarket launch timeline).
EVIDENCE:
  1. DARPA PAM documentation (DTIC AD1052571): PAM designed as combinatorial prediction market for geopolitical events in 8 Middle Eastern nations under IAO / source: https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD1052571.pdf / falsifier: If PAM was not a DARPA/IAO prediction market program, claim fails.
  2. Robin Hanson futarchy paper (2013): "Shall We Vote on Values, But Bet on Beliefs?" — Hanson proposes governance by prediction markets / source: https://mason.gmu.edu/~rhanson/futarchy2013.pdf / falsifier: If Hanson never proposed futarchy, claim weakens.
  3. NYMag Coplan profile (archived): Coplan emailed Hanson 2019 with "verbose email with multiple block paragraphs that he wanted 'to be the person to bring prediction markets to life'" / source: https://web.archive.org/web/20250311093118/https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/is-polymarket-legal-politics-betting-shayne-coplan.html / falsifier: If Coplan never contacted Hanson, claim fails.
  4. Polymarket launch June 2020: PRNewswire announcement / source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/polymarket-raises-70m-from-thiels-founders-fund-general-catalyst-vitalik-buterin-to-scale-global-prediction-market-302555123.html / falsifier: If Polymarket launch date ≠ 2020, claim weakens.
  5. Unlimited Hangout Part 1 (Webb/Goodwin 2026): Documents PAM→Polymarket lineage, Hanson-Coplan contact, Thiel funding / source: https://unlimitedhangout.com/2026/06/investigative-series/the-secret-history-of-polymarket-part-1/ / falsifier: If article doesn't document this lineage, claim weakens.
ARGUMENT: PAM was the Pentagon's first major prediction market for geopolitical forecasting. It was shut down by Congress in 2003. The same system architect (Hanson) was directly contacted by Coplan before Polymarket's launch. The architectural continuity (combinatorial markets, geopolitical focus, futarchy governance vision) plus direct personal contact plus Thiel-network funding establishes direct descent.
