# CL-005 — CFTC capture: Selig/Quintenz/Andreessen Horowitz link Kalshi and Polymarket

**DESCRIPTION:** During Trump's 2nd term, the CFTC chair is Michael Selig, "previously a lawyer representing major cryptocurrency industry clients." Selig only got the role after the Trump administration rescinded the nomination of Brian Quintenz, who was on Kalshi's board and worked for "the crypto venture team at Marc Andreessen's firm Andreessen Horowitz." Andreessen Horowitz has "notably backed Coplan's new prediction market VC firm 5c(c) Capital and is also heavily invested in Kalshi." 5c(c) Capital was founded by Coplan and Tarek Mansour (Kalshi CEO) — i.e. the "rivals" buried the hatchet and now share a venture fund. Donald Trump Jr. is "both a strategic advisor to Kalshi and also advises Polymarket" and is a major investor via 1789 Capital.
