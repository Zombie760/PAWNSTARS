# CL-006 — Augur → Polymarket "Napsterization" via Joey Krug at Founders Fund

**DESCRIPTION:** Augur (Ethereum-based prediction market) was co-founded in 2014 by Joey Krug (Thiel Fellow), with Vitalik Buterin (Thiel Fellow) and Robin Hanson (PAM architect) as advisors. Augur's logo is a "one-eyed pyramid" — explicitly designed to evoke the logos of DARPA's TIA and PAM programs. Augur raised via the first ICO on Ethereum in Fall 2015. Krug joined Dan Morehead's Pantera Capital as co-CIO in 2017, then joined Peter Thiel's Founders Fund in April 2023 with Sean Parker to "shape the next decade of Founders Fund's crypto strategy." In December 2025, Krug said: "maybe Augur is Napster and Polymarket is Spotify." Krug led a $45M Polymarket investment round in 2024 after Coplan hit the "$5M a week" target Krug had set for him.
