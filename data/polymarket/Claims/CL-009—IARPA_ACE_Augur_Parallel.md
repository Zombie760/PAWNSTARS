# CL-009 — IARPA ACE program parallel to Augur (2011-2015)

**DESCRIPTION:** Three years before renewed interest in crypto prediction markets, DARPA's sister agency IARPA (Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity) launched "a four-year, $50 million program" called **Aggregative Contingent Estimation (ACE)** to "pay people willing to predict major world events." ACE was headed by Jason Matheny. After ACE, Matheny ran IARPA and then launched the Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET) with a $55M grant from the Open Philanthropy Project (co-founded by Dustin Moskovitz, Facebook co-founder). The ACE program "officially ended in June 2015, shortly after Krug formalized the non-profit entity tied to Augur development — the Forecast Foundation." Augur co-founder Jeremy Gardner posted in 2015 that some on the Augur team were "speaking to a few several-letter agencies" to help them understand the technology, and that "the DoD, DARPA, and CIA are huge fans of Augur."

**FALSIFIER:** If ACE program didn't exist or wasn't $50M, or Matheny didn't head it, or Augur/ACE timeline doesn't match (ACE end June 2015, Forecast Foundation founded Dec 2014), claim fails. If Gardner's "several-letter agencies" post doesn't exist on archived web, claim weakens.
