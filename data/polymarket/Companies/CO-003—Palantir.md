# CO-003 — Palantir

**DESCRIPTION:** "Engine on which the surveillance state runs." Peter Thiel co-founded May 2003 (same month TIA renamed "Terrorism Information Awareness"). Architects: Thiel, Alex Karp, Stephen Cohen, Joe Lonsdale. Originally an anti-fraud algorithm called Igor from PayPal. **CIA In-Q-Tel first backer (~$2M, publicly disclosed mid-2006).** Richard Perle introduced Thiel/Karp to John Poindexter. CIA CIO Alan Wade key In-Q-Tel figure. **CIA was sole client 2003-2008.** Engineer Aki Jain made 200+ trips to CIA HQ 2005-2009. Now contractor to all 18 US IC agencies. Major defense contractor under Trump 1st and 2nd terms. **CEO Alex Karp** admitted fear of "far right" killing him. "We built PG [proprietary software], which single-handedly stopped the rise of the far-right in Europe" (WEF 2023). Palantir co-founder Joe Lonsdale is "a key fixture" at 2019 Event 201 pandemic simulation. **Tiberius platform** used for Trump-era COVID vaccine allocation, HHS Protect, demographic targeting.

**PALANTIR TECH:** Selective revelation + immutable log = direct TIA borrow.
