# CO-001 — Polymarket

**DESCRIPTION:** World's largest crypto-powered prediction market. Public face: Shayne Coplan, world's youngest "self-made" billionaire. **Direct ancestor: DARPA's Policy Analysis Market (PAM)** — Coplan contacted PAM architect Robin Hanson in 2019 with "verbose email" about "bringing prediction markets to life." Predecessor: TokenUnion (2017) which "has seemingly been erased from current narratives" — domains tokenbnk.com, tokenunion.io, union.market, poly.market, polymarket.com all now redirect to polymarket.com. Thiel network funding: Sean Parker (Founders Fund partner), Joey Krug (led $45M Founders Fund round 2024). Media partnerships: Wall Street Journal/Dow Jones, Yahoo! Finance, Substack, Parti. "Polymarket's odds now capable of moving the stock market, manipulating the media and even influencing elections." Trump Jr. is advisor + investor via 1789 Capital.

**LAUNCHED:** June 2020. "Built in his bathroom." (Per official narrative.)
**BACKERS:** Founders Fund ($45M round 2024), Vitalik Buterin, General Catalyst, 1789 Capital (Don Jr.), 5c(c) Capital (Coplan + Mansour + a16z).
**LEGAL:** Built on Polygon L2, USDC stablecoin, oracle: UMA. CFTC-regulated via 5c(c) Capital VC arm.
