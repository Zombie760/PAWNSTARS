# CO-002 — Augur

**DESCRIPTION:** First Ethereum-based prediction market platform. **First resurrection of PAM.** Co-founded 2014 by Joey Krug (Thiel Fellow), Jeremy Gardner, Jack Peterson. Inspired by Paul Sztorc's TruthCoin whitepaper (Dec 2013). Advised by Vitalik Buterin (Thiel Fellow) and Robin Hanson (PAM architect). Logo deliberately a "one-eyed pyramid" with "three points converging upon an all-seeing eye" — directly evoking TIA/PAM. **Forecast Foundation (Estonia, Dec 2014)** non-profit. First ICO on Ethereum (REP token), Fall 2015. ICO "courted four major banks" + partnered with IBM Watson. Augur co-founder Jeremy Gardner (2015): Augur team "speaking to a few several-letter agencies," "DoD, DARPA, and CIA are huge fans of Augur." Tradesports.com leased technology to Forecast Foundation in exchange for REP tokens. Krug awarded Thiel Fellowship June 2016. Joined Pantera Capital 2017. Augur v1 July 2018. Augur v2 2020 (replaced REP with DAI). Augur Turbo 2021 (partnered with Chainlink, Polygon). Krug left Augur in messy lawsuit, joined Founders Fund April 2023. Krug Dec 2025: "maybe Augur is Napster and Polymarket is Spotify."
