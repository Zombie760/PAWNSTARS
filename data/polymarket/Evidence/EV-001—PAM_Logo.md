# EV-001 — DARPA PAM Logo (Pyramid in "A")

**ARTIFACT:** Archived PAM website shows the logo: pyramid (one-eyed, surveilling Earth) embedded in letter "A" with a beam from the pyramid's opposite side touching a market graph.

**LOCATION:** Unlimted Hangout, Part 1, "DARPA Discovers Prediction Markets" section. Image archived at https://unlimitedhangout.com/2026/06/investigative-series/the-secret-history-of-polymarket-part-1/

**RELEVANCE:** Direct visual evidence of PAM-TIA visual lineage (both one-eyed pyramid logos). Web article notes: "The IAO/TIA logo, which has since become infamous, shows a one-eyed pyramid surveilling the entire Earth... The PAM logo places the pyramid within the letter A and that pyramid, like the TIA pyramid, is also surveilling the Earth."

**FALSIFIER:** If no archived PAM website shows this logo, or logos are different, claim fails.
