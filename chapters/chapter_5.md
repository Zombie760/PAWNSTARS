### 5.1.2 PAM Design

PAM was a **futures market for geopolitical events** — terrorist attacks, regime changes, WMD proliferation. Traders bet on outcomes; market prices = probability estimates.

| Market Category | Examples |
|----------------|----------|
| **Terrorism** | Attack on US soil, WMD use |
| **Regime Change** | Assad falls, Kim Jong-un removed |
| **Economic** | Oil price > $X, currency collapse |

**F-PAM**: PAM never existed, or budget < $1M, or Hanson not architect.
**Receipts**: `R-PAM-001` through `R-PAM-005`.
