Abstract

This dissertation demonstrates that the companies commonly described as "Silicon Valley startups" — Palantir, Facebook/Meta, SpaceX/Starlink, Google, Polymarket, and Anduril — are not market-emergent ventures. They are intelligence program privatizations executed through a documented, repeatable procurement pipeline. This chapter establishes the theoretical framework: "intelligence program privatization" as a distinct procurement category with a documented 5-stage pipeline, identifies the selectorate and operators who execute it, and states the central thesis with 10 falsifiable claims.

1.1 The Puzzle

In 2003, DARPA's Total Information Awareness (TIA) program was defunded by Congress amid privacy outcry. In May 2003 — the same month — Palantir Technologies incorporated. Its first client: the CIA. Its seed investor: In-Q-Tel, the CIA's venture capital arm. Its architectural inspiration: TIA's core data-fusion concepts. sources:[https://www.darpa.mil/about-us/legacy-projects/total-information-awareness, https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_de/3759318, https://www.iqt.org/portfolio/palantir-technologies/]

In 2004, DARPA's LifeLog program — designed to capture "the totality of a person's experience" — was publicly cancelled on February 4. That same day, TheFacebook.com launched. Its first outside investor: Peter Thiel (Palantir co-founder, In-Q-Tel portfolio). Its president: Sean Parker (CIA-recruited at 16). sources:[https://www.wired.com/2004/02/darpa-lifelog-project-shut-down/, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312509150865/ds1.htm, https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexknapp/2011/09/22/sean-parker-the-billionaire-who-was-recruited-by-the-cia-at-16/]

In 2003, DARPA's Policy Analysis Market (PAM) — a prediction market for geopolitical events — was shut down by a 96-0 Senate vote. Its architect, Robin Hanson, invented "futarchy" ("vote on values, bet on beliefs"). In 2014, Augur launched as the first Ethereum prediction market, co-founded by Thiel Fellow Joey Krug. In 2020, Polymarket launched, backed by Founders Fund, with Thiel Fellow Joey Krug as advisor. sources:[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/2003/07/22/senate-section/article/S10575-1, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.1898, https://thiefellowship.org/fellows/joey-krug/, https://www.unlimitedhangout.com/2026/06/investigations/the-secret-history-of-polymarket-part-1/]

In 2006, NASA Administrator Mike Griffin — former In-Q-Tel President — awarded SpaceX its first major contract (COTS, $278M). In 2021, SpaceX won a $1.8B classified NRO contract (Starshield). sources:[https://www.iqt.org/team/mike-griffin/, https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/station/main/cots-program.html, https://www.reuters.com/technology/space/musks-spacex-building-spy-satellite-network-us-intelligence-sources-2024-03-16/]

The pattern is not coincidence. It is a procurement pipeline.
